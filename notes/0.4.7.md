## Stats and awards

Your own service record, counted by the Reborn servers from the game itself.

- **Where:** STATS & AWARDS on the main menu.
- **What it shows:** your rank and the next one, then eight numbers: kills, deaths, K/D, score, flags, rounds won,
  headshots and repairs. Below them come your time in each kit and vehicle, your results on each map and your latest
  awards.
- **What is counted:** kills, deaths, team kills, suicides, headshots, melee kills, assists, flags captured and
  defended, repairs, heals, score, rounds, wins and losses, time in each kit and vehicle, kills from vehicles, results
  by map and your best kill streak.
- **Where it counts:** only on Reborn servers. Totals are kept for all time, this month and this week.
- **New players:** deaths, K/D and rounds won show a dash until a Reborn server has counted you.

## Leaderboards

- **Where:** LEADERBOARDS on the main menu.
- **Boards:** Score, Kills, K/D, Flags and Time. Switch between them with LB and RB.
- **Periods:** X switches between all time, this month and this week.
- **Your place:** your own row stays pinned under the table, wherever you are on it.
- **K/D:** you need 50 kills to appear on the K/D board.

## Achievements

- **Where:** ACHIEVEMENTS on the main menu.
- **The list:** all 12 of the game's achievements. Locked ones are grey, and each shows your progress where it can be
  counted, plus how many players have it. X filters all, unlocked or locked.
- **Unlocking one:** a slip slides in at the top right for five seconds, with a chime.
- **Settings:** Settings > AUDIO has "Achievement popups" (on) and "Achievement sound volume" (80%).
- **Your own chime:** put a 16 bit WAV named achievement.wav in your user folder's sounds folder and it plays instead.

## Clan tags

Wear your clan's tag in front of your name: [TAG]Name.

- **Where:** your name page (EDIT on the main menu, or the name in Settings) now has a CLAN TAG box before SOLDIER
  NAME. RB, F4 or a click moves between the two.
- **Rules:** 2 to 4 letters or digits, upper and lower case kept, or leave it blank.
- **Where it shows:** the main menu, the pause scoreboard, leaderboards, friends, chat, the names of players who are
  talking, and in the match: the kill feed, the name tags and the scoreboard.
- **Not allowed:** tags that pretend to be the developers or staff, swear words, and a tag that spells something
  rude together with your name. The page tells you why when a tag is refused.
- **[Dev]:** only the Reborn developers wear the Dev tag, and theirs cycles through colours so you can always tell it
  is real.

## Protect your name

Keep your soldier name, rank and stats as yours, with no email and no password.

- **Where:** your name page, NAME PROTECTION. Press X (F2 on a keyboard) to protect your name.
- **Your recovery code:** you get a 20 character code. It shows once on the page and is also saved as "BF1943 Reborn
  recovery code.txt" in your user folder. Keep it somewhere safe. X shows or hides it again later.
- **On a new PC:** press LB (F3), choose USE A RECOVERY CODE and type your code. Your name and progress come back.
- **Someone else's name:** if a name is protected, anyone else using it plays online with a number added.
- **Limits:** a name not played for 180 days is freed for others. It needs the Reborn online service.

## Team balance

On Reborn servers, teams no longer stay lopsided.

- **When:** one team has 2 or more players more than the other, with at least 4 players on the server, for 20
  seconds.
- **How:** the bigger team gets a warning, then players who are down (waiting to spawn) are moved one at a time.
- **No penalty:** you lose no kills, no score and no tickets, and your score comes with you.
- **Who moves first:** a player who used Change Team to stack a side, then a player in no squad, then the newest
  arrival. Nobody is moved twice in 15 minutes, and nobody is moved between rounds.
- **Change Team:** a switch that would leave the teams 2 or more apart is refused.
- **What you see:** a chat line from the server, and if you are moved, a box saying which side you are on now and
  that no points were lost.
- **Scoreboard:** the team names now follow your team after any switch, on the end of round screen too.
- **Running your own server:** it is on by default for managed servers (the "Team balance" box) and off for private
  and LAN matches.

## Report an issue

Found a bug? Send it from inside the game.

- **Where:** REPORT AN ISSUE on the main menu.
- **Step 1, your report:** pick what kind it is (Crash, Bug, Performance, Server or connection, Suggestion), give it
  a title and say what happened. You can also add steps to make it happen again, what you expected, how often it
  happens and a screenshot. Your draft is kept until you send or clear it.
- **Step 2, what is attached:** everything that goes with the report is listed with a tick, so you choose: the game
  and PC details, your display and graphics settings, the last match (never its address), a crash report, the end of
  the log, your newest F12 screenshot and a way to contact you (off unless you turn it on). Your user name, PC name,
  addresses, recovery code, keys and email addresses are removed before anything is sent.
- **Step 3, send it:** send it straight from the game with no GitHub account, open it on GitHub with your own
  account, or copy it.
- **Offline:** a report that could not be sent is kept and tried again at the next start.
- **Controller:** A opens the on screen keys, RB goes to the next step. **Keyboard:** Tab for the next field,
  Ctrl+Enter for the next step.
- **Linux:** the form works, but screenshots can only be attached on Windows for now.

## Controls by device

- **Where:** Settings > CONTROLS now opens on two cards: KEYBOARD & MOUSE or CONTROLLER. The one you used last is
  picked for you.
- **Only what you need:** each page shows only its own settings. Keyboard and mouse: input, mouse, invert and
  bindings, with two keys and two mouse buttons for every action. Controller: input, look, invert, aim, vibration,
  the game's layouts and buttons.
- **The picture:** the help card draws your device with the chosen key or button lit up, with pictures for the Xbox
  Series, Xbox 360, DualSense, DualShock 3 and Steam Deck controllers.

## Button prompts and menu sounds

- **New prompts:** the button pictures in the launcher, menus and HUD are now Kenney's Input Prompts.
  "Button prompts" offers Automatic, Xbox 360, Xbox Series, PlayStation 3, PlayStation 5, Steam Deck, and
  keyboard and mouse. The Xbox 360 style keeps the game's own pictures.
- **Original menu sounds:** the launcher, menus, pause menu and on screen keys now play the original menus' four
  sounds, made from your own game files (none are shipped with Reborn). Settings > AUDIO has "Menu sounds" (on) and
  "Menu sound volume" (100%).
- **Custom Match:** on a controller, A on Server name or Password now opens the on screen keys.

## News window

What you are reading started here: a news window on the main menu with the state of the Reborn servers, our news,
what is coming up and our videos.

- **When:** it opens once each time you start the game. The NEWS row on the main menu opens it again and shows how
  many items are new.
- **Controller:** move with the stick, D pad or LB and RB, A to open or watch, Y to mute, B to close.
- **Keyboard:** arrows or Q and E, Enter, R to mute, Esc to close.
- **Videos:** play inside the game on Windows, with sound. The menu music waits while a video plays. On Linux and the
  Steam Deck you get a picture, and A opens the video on YouTube.

## Discord status

- **What:** your Discord profile shows what you are doing in Reborn: in the menus, browsing servers, joining, or in a
  match with the map, mode, team, server name, player count and match time.
- **Privacy:** it never shows a server address or another player's name.
- **Setting:** Settings > GAMEPLAY > "Discord status" (on). It needs the Discord app running on the same PC.

## Performance and memory

Measured on a Ryzen 7 7800X3D with an RTX 4070 Ti.

- **Less memory:** about 340 MB less graphics memory (2,085 MB down to 1,745 MB), and about 190 MB less system
  memory in a match.
- **Unlimited means unlimited:** every frame rate is offered whatever your screen. VSync off no longer holds you to
  the refresh rate (236 FPS on a 120 Hz screen), and VSync on really syncs.
- **Fewer cores, smoother play:** on PCs with few cores the game sleeps instead of spinning while it waits. On two
  cores, uncapped, the frame rate went from 131 to about 200 and the 1% low from 28 to over 90.
- **Lighter on the whole PC:** all the game's threads together use less processor time at the same frame rate.
- **A calmer launcher:** the menus run at 60 FPS and drop to 30 after 10 seconds idle. Graphics card load while idle
  went from about 35% to 13%. A minimised game holds itself to 30 FPS.
- **Future frame rendering:** a new choice in Settings > DISPLAY (off by default). It renders a frame ahead for a
  higher frame rate, but adds input lag.
- **Faster start:** the window opens about a second sooner on PCs with many display modes.
- **Audio crackling fixed.**
- **Older processors:** a PC whose processor is too old (Core 2, Phenom II) now gets a clear message instead of the
  game closing silently.
- **Smaller download:** the Visual C++ runtime now comes with the game, and the package is 161 MB smaller.
- **System requirements:** a processor with SSE4.2, Vulkan, 8 GB of memory, Windows 10 (1607 or newer) or Windows 11,
  and about 1 GB of video memory at Potato or Low (1.5 to 1.7 GB with everything up).

## Linux and Steam Deck

- **More distributions:** the AppImage now starts on Ubuntu 22.04, Linux Mint 21, Pop!_OS 22.04, Zorin 17, Debian 12
  and anything newer.
- **Wayland:** the window is drawn at its real pixel size.
- **Faster:** a leaner build, and quicker memory lookups on newer kernels (6.11 and up).

## Servers

- **Maintenance notices:** when the servers are closed for maintenance, the main menu and the server browser say
  SERVERS CLOSED with the reason, and joining shows the notice. Players already in a match are not affected.
- **Full servers:** joining a full server is refused cleanly ("SERVER FULL", with how many players it has) instead of
  causing trouble on the server.
- **Fewer server crashes:** three kinds of server crash are now caught and passed over, so a match keeps going instead
  of dropping everyone.
- **Linux servers:** a true 60 ticks a second (one had been running at 55).
- **Reborn servers:** the server browser's tabs are now Reborn, Community, Favourites, LAN and Direct connect, and our
  servers carry a REBORN badge.
- **General bug fixes.**

## Smaller fixes

- **Deploy map:** clicking a spawn point picks it once, and the markers line up with where you click on every map
  (they were off on Iwo Jima and Guadalcanal).
- **Shaders:** the shader list grew to 3,015 pipelines from players' uploads, so fewer effects stall or pop in the
  first time you see them.
- **Launch options:** an option given twice no longer drops all the others.
